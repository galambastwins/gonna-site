Terms of Use

About Gonna

Gonna helps groups plan and share adventures: dates and places, plans, packing lists, files and shared expenses. Gonna is made by Galambas Studio, a small independent studio in Portugal run by Guilherme Galambas. Galambas Studio is not a company, so these terms are the agreement between you and Guilherme Galambas when you use the app. By creating an account you accept them.

Gonna is free to use.

Your account

You sign in with Apple or Google. You need to be at least 16, or older if your country sets a higher age for agreeing to this on your own. Keep that Apple or Google account safe: whoever controls it can use your Gonna account. One account is for one person.

Adventures and groups

When you create or join an adventure, the other members can see what is shared in it: its name, dates, cover, places, plans, packing lists, shared files, links, expenses and who is in the group. They also see your name, username and photo. Only share what you are happy for the group to see.

Details from your profile, like your passport number, and files you keep in your profile reach an adventure only when you choose to share them. You can stop sharing at any time.

An invitation link lets anyone who has it see the adventure's name, cover and dates, and join. Share it only with people you want in the group. You can also add someone by name before they join. Only add people who are fine with that.

Expenses and payments

Gonna keeps a record of who paid what and who owes whom. It doesn't move money or process payments. Recording a payment in Gonna doesn't mean money changed hands. Settle up between yourselves. Currency conversions use the European Central Bank's reference rates and are a guide only.

What you add

You keep the rights to what you add (text, photos, files). You let us store, process and show it to the people you share it with, only so the app can work. You are responsible for having the right to share what you add.

Fair use

Don't use Gonna to break the law, harass people, share content that isn't yours to share, or try to access other people's data. Don't try to break, overload or reverse-engineer the service. We can suspend or close accounts that do. Where we can, we'll tell you why first.

The service

Gonna is offered as it is, and is still being developed. We work to keep it available and your data safe, but we can't promise it will always be available or free of errors. Weather forecasts, maps, exchange rates and other information from third parties may be wrong. Check anything important.

To the extent the law allows, we aren't liable for indirect losses, or for losses caused by information from third parties or by what other members do. We stay liable where the law says we must, such as for intent or gross negligence. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have under consumer law.

Ending and disputes

You can delete your account at any time from Settings. We may change these terms. If a change matters, we'll tell you in the app at least 15 days before it applies. If you don't agree, you can delete your account. If you keep using Gonna after that, the new terms apply.

Portuguese law applies to these terms. If you live in another country, you keep the consumer protections of that country and you can bring a claim in its courts.

Questions or complaints: gonna@galambas.studio. These terms were last updated on 26 November 2026.

Privacy Policy

Who is responsible

Guilherme Galambas, who runs Galambas Studio in Portugal, is the controller of your personal data in Gonna. Contact: gonna@galambas.studio.

What we store

  • Account: the identifier from Apple or Google, and the email and name they give us (with Apple, this can be a private relay address).
  • Profile: your name, username and photo. If you choose to add them: birthday, citizen card number, tax number and passport number.
  • Adventures: names, descriptions, dates, cover photos, places and routes, plans, packing lists, links (like a group chat or a shared album), who is going, people added by name, invitations, and which places you visited.
  • Files: files and document photos you add. They are encrypted on your phone with a new key for each file before they are uploaded. We keep the key on our servers, separately, so the app can open the file for you and for the people you share it with. This is not end-to-end encryption.
  • Expenses: amounts, currencies, who paid, how they are split, and payments between members.
  • Notifications: your notification preferences, the updates in your feed, group alerts you send and, if you allow push notifications, your device's push token.
  • Calendar: if you subscribe to an adventure's calendar, a private link. Anyone who has that link can read the adventure's calendar until you turn it off.
  • On this phone only: your appearance, default currency and maps app, your sign-in session, and expenses waiting to be sent while you are offline.

There are no ads and no analytics tools in Gonna. We don't track you across other apps, we don't sell your data, and we don't make automated decisions about you.

Permissions on your phone

  • Camera and photos: only when you take or choose a photo (a cover, a profile photo, a document).
  • Calendar and contacts: only when you open a plan in your calendar. The iPhone's own editor handles it. Gonna doesn't read your calendar or upload your contacts.
  • Notifications: only if you allow them.

We don't use your phone's location.

Who sees it

  • Members of an adventure see what is shared in it, and your name, username and photo.
  • Your private profile details stay private until you share them with an adventure. Its members then see them, kept up to date, until you stop sharing.
  • Files in your profile are only yours, unless you share one with an adventure.
  • People on Gonna can find you by name or username to invite you. They see your name, username and photo.
  • Anyone with an invitation link sees the adventure's name, cover and dates, and who invited them.

We hold the file keys, so we could technically open a file. We only look at your data when we need to keep the service working or when the law requires it.

Who helps us

  • Supabase runs our database, sign-in, file storage and server code, in the European Union (Ireland).
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, push notifications (Apple receives your device's push token and the alert text), weather forecasts (WeatherKit receives the coordinates and dates of a place) and place search (Apple receives what you type when you search for a place).
  • Google: Sign in with Google, if you use it.
  • Maps: when you open a place in a maps app, its name and address go to the app you chose (Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze).
  • Exchange rates come from the European Central Bank. No personal data is sent.
  • Links you open, like a group chat, a shared album or a weather page, go to those services under their own policies.

Some of these companies may handle data outside the European Union. When they do, they use safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Why we use it

  • To give you the app you signed up for. That is our contract with you.
  • For the optional profile details and push notifications, because you agreed. You can remove them or turn them off at any time.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse. That is our legitimate interest.
  • To meet a legal duty, when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. You can remove most of it yourself at any time.

When you delete your account, we remove your profile, private details, files, push tokens and access to adventures. This is permanent. Expenses and payments you were part of stay in the group's history under an unnamed participant that is no longer linked to your account, so everyone's balances stay right. People who were in the group may still remember who it was.

If you organise an adventure that still has other members, you choose a new organiser before deleting. If you are the last member, the adventure is archived.

When the last member leaves an adventure, its plans, links, shared files, shared details, calendar links and shared packing lists are removed. The expense record is kept.

Backups may keep deleted data for a limited time before they are overwritten.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your data, to move it elsewhere, to object to how we use it, or to limit it. Where we rely on your agreement, you can withdraw it at any time.

You can do most of this in the app. For anything else, write to gonna@galambas.studio. We answer within one month.

You can also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the CNPD (cnpd.pt), or to the authority in your country.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Files and document photos are encrypted before they leave your phone. We hold the keys, so this protects them in storage but is not end-to-end encryption. Access is checked on the server, so people only see the adventures they belong to. No system is perfectly safe. If a breach puts you at risk, we'll tell you.

Children

Gonna isn't meant for anyone under 16. If you think a child has an account, tell us and we'll remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before it applies. This policy was last updated on 26 November 2026.
